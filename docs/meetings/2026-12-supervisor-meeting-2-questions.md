# Supervisor meeting 2: questions

Questions to raise at the second supervisor meeting, planned for the end of December 2026. They come from building the study system after [meeting 1](2026-11-supervisor-meeting-1.md) and from the open decisions in [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md). The study design questions affect the study itself, so send them by email before the study if it runs before the meeting.

## Study design

1. **Prompt suggestions in the AI condition:** "Suggest a prompt" can name the drawn object with a local vision model (Florence-2) or fill fixed style templates. Which should participants get, and does either one count as AI help that the no-AI condition must also lack?
2. **Same task twice:** every participant draws each task with and without AI, so the second drawing benefits from the first. Is that acceptable, or should each participant draw half the tasks in each condition? If they draw every task in both conditions, should the condition order alternate between participants?
3. **Time limit:** should each drawing have a fixed time, or end when the participant says they are done?
4. **Instructions:** how much should participants be told about the suggestions, and must they press "Suggest next strokes" at least once in an AI drawing?
5. **Participants and consent:** is a consent form or ethics approval needed, and should any background about the participants be recorded, such as drawing experience?

## Evaluation

6. **Experts:** how many experts rate the drawings, on what criteria and scale, and do they see only the final drawings or also the process captures?
7. **Following suggestions:** the system infers whether each suggestion was followed. Should that follow rate be a study result, and should part of it be checked against hand labels?

## Publication and report

8. **Conference:** which venue, which deadline in early 2027, and what does the submission need from the system and the data?
9. **Final report:** what are the guidelines, the length, and the deadline?
10. **Thesis extension:** when should the 40 drawings from professional artists be collected, and do they follow the same protocol?

## Project

11. **License:** the repository is public. Is there a university policy for the software license?
12. **Continuing from a candidate (U2):** is continuing the next round from a selected generated image still required, now that guided sketching is the focus?
